🪂 Paragliding Flight Log Engine

Paragliding Flight Log is a professional-grade personal flight telemetry, scoring, and analysis platform built natively for Google Sheets and Google Apps Script.

Designed for thermal soaring, cross-country (XC), and hike-and-fly pilots, it combines instant IGC parsing, coordinate geo-enrichment, weather telemetry, deduplication scoring, and dynamic dashboards.
